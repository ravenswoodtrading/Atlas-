Your main manual search tool -- type a brand or upload an ASIN list, Atlas checks Keepa UK plus all 4 EU marketplaces, and lists every resulting opportunity with score/profit/ROI.

Score (0-100) is how good the opportunity is. Confidence (0-100) is how much to trust that score -- docked for price swings, offer-count spikes, or thin sales evidence. BUY needs score 85+ and confidence 80+; CONSIDER needs 65+/60+. Green rows have confirmed sales evidence and over 25% ROI (today's or the 90-day typical price) -- see the star buy definition.

An ASIN scanned in the last 24 hours is skipped by default to save Keepa tokens -- use "Force rescan" to override. Products get filtered out before ever spending an EU token if Amazon's own UK fees alone already eat the whole price (a "never viable" ceiling check), or if they look dead (no sales-rank drops and no current offers in 30 days). A red GATED badge means a real opportunity you're currently blocked from selling -- see Excluded vs. Gated.

"Today" vs. "90d" figures: today's live price vs. the typical price over the last 90 days -- Atlas shows whichever is better, so a temporary discount doesn't hide a normally-good product. See "Today" vs "90d" vs "Peak".
