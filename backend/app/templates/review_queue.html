Your single unified action list, merging two different systems (2026-08-24): Atlas's own deterministic scan scoring (star buys, BUY/CONSIDER/PEAK, competitor finds) AND the AI-judged leads from the Verdict Checker and Shortlist (BUY/WATCH verdicts, with a written rationale under "Why?"). Same page, same thumbs up/down habit, whichever system found it.

Buy / Star buys tab: confirmed sales evidence and over 25% ROI (today's or the 90-day typical price) -- see star buy. A PEAK lead only makes it into this tab if its peak ROI is 35%+ or its score is 65+ -- a stricter bar than what earned it the PEAK tag in the first place (10% peak ROI), so some weaker PEAK opportunities are filtered out before you ever see them. A BUY-verdict lead lands here too.

Consider tab: profitable leads (today's or 90-day price) with some sign of real sales, but not clearing the stricter star-buy bar above -- plus any WATCH-verdict lead. This list isn't meant to be cleared to zero -- browse it as you have time. An AVOID-verdict lead is excluded from both tabs entirely, same treatment IGNORE already gets.

A Conflict badge means the two systems disagree on this exact ASIN -- e.g. Atlas's scan pipeline marks it IGNORE while a Verdict Check came back BUY, or vice versa. Worth a second look before acting either way; Atlas doesn't pick a side for you.

"Sourcing reasoning" numbers on competitor-sourced rows are Atlas's best GUESS at how a competitor sourced something, inferred from price history alone -- never a confirmed fact. Reviewing (thumbs up/down) removes a lead from whichever tab it's in. Verdict Checker/Shortlist leads don't yet support the bulk checkbox actions -- use their own thumbs up/down/OOS buttons.
