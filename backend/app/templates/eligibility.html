Check a Keepa export

You get the same file back with three columns added at the end:

  • Eligible: Y (you can sell it), N (restricted) or Unknown (Amazon didn't answer)
  • Restriction reason: e.g. APPROVAL_REQUIRED, when it's restricted
  • Checked at: when Amazon last answered

Products checked in the last week or two are instant. New ones take about half a second each, so a big fresh export can take 30–40 minutes. The page shows an estimate once it starts.

Starting from barcodes instead? Look up EAN/UPC codes on Amazon, then check them the same way.

A barcode that matches no product, or more than one, is flagged for you to check by hand.
