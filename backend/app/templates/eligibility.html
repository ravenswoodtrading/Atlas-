Keepa export

Upload a Keepa Product Finder export (CSV or XLSX) that has an ASIN column. You get the same file back, with every original column and row kept and three columns added:

  • Eligible: Y, N or Unknown (couldn't check)
  • Restriction reason: e.g. APPROVAL_REQUIRED. Blank unless restricted.
  • Checked at: when Amazon answered (UTC). Older for cached answers.

Barcodes (EAN / UPC)

For retailer-first sourcing: paste barcodes, or upload a CSV/XLSX with an EAN, UPC, GTIN or Barcode column. Atlas finds the Amazon UK ASIN(s) for each one and checks those. A barcode that matches no ASIN, or more than one, is flagged for you to check.
