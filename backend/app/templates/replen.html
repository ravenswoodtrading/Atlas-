Every ASIN we have ever bought Amazon-to-Amazon from Germany, France, Spain or Italy, read straight from the Buy Sheet. It is not limited to things that have already sold through: something that wasn't worth repeating last time may be a winner now, and something you bought last week and is already selling is exactly what you want to see.

Each row is judged from three live sources:

  • Buy Sheet — what you paid per unit, what you planned to sell it for, when, and how many.
  • Amazon (SP-API) — units in stock and inbound, and units shipped to customers in the last 30 days. Free; refreshed every day.
  • Keepa — today's cheapest EU cost, the UK Buy Box and the resulting ROI (today's, not the best of today and 90 days). Rolling: about {{ daily_pct }}% of the list is re-priced each day, so every ASIN is re-priced roughly every four days. Items bought in the last 30 days, or that shipped units recently, are re-priced sooner.

Verdicts

  • Buy more ROI is {{ "%.0f"|format(buy_roi) }}% or better and there isn't a pile of stock already. You get a Discord ping when something flips into this (not for ones that were already there).
  • Well stocked profitable, but you already have more than about {{ cover_days_max }} days of stock at the current sales rate, or none has shipped in 30 days.
  • Thin margin viable (ROI 17–{{ "%.0f"|format(buy_roi) }}%) but not a clear buy.
  • Wait not profitable now — and the reason is shown: the EU cost is up on what you paid, the UK Buy Box is down on what you planned, or both. A 10% move either way is treated as noise.
  • No EU source no EU marketplace has it at a usable price right now.
  • Dead / gated nobody's selling it, Keepa has no record, it's in an excluded category, or Amazon has it gated for us.

Finding things

  • Quick views are one-click shortcuts, e.g. Buy more & sold out is what's worth reordering with nothing left on the shelf.
  • Verdict chips and the filter bar combine: every filter narrows the others, and the chip counts show what you'd get if you clicked them right now. Filters stay put when you sort, change page, hide an item or re-price one.
  • Click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse. Rows with no value for that column always go last. Click a brand name in a row to show only that brand. The little × on a filter tag removes just that filter.
  • "Since we bought" compares today's EU cost and UK Buy Box with what you paid and planned last time; a 10% move counts.

Hide (the eye) stops an ASIN being re-priced or alerted on; it stays in the Buy Sheet history. The old Replen list (proven winners from sales exports) has been replaced; its data is still in the database.
