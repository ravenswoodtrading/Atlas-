1. Sales Summary by SKU

Source: SellerToolKit, sales reporting/export area.

Export sales totals grouped by SKU, for your UK/GBP business. Select the full reporting date range and include all products.

Required: SKU, ASIN, Units, Profit_Loss, Sales, CoG, RoI, Margin, Current_Stock_QTY. Include Title for product names.

One row per SKU across the selected period. This feeds the overall SKU performance view.

2. Daily Sales by SKU

Source: SellerToolKit, sales reporting/export area.

Export the daily breakdown grouped by date and SKU, with the same UK/GBP scope and date range. Include all products.

Required: Date, SKU, ASIN, Units, Profit_Loss, Sales and CoG. Keep the original column headers and total amounts for each row.

This supplies sales-line drill-down, sell-through timing and allocated ROI/margin. “Daily” describes the rows; it is not an upload schedule.

3. Amazon FBA Inventory Ledger — Detailed View

Where to get it: sign in to Amazon Seller Central UK, then open Reports → Fulfilment → Inventory → Inventory Ledger. Select Detailed View, the UK marketplace, and the range from before the earliest VA purchase through the latest date you want to reconcile. Download as TXT so ASIN, FNSKU and MSKU formatting is preserved.

Why Atlas needs it: it records receipts, customer shipments, returns, transfers, lost/damaged/disposed units and ending balances. This lets Atlas explain what happened to units that are not matched by the SellerToolKit sales export.

This is a manual upload for now. Atlas stores the ledger centrally for VA Actuals and future inventory reconciliation.

These are Atlas’s descriptive report names. SellerToolKit’s exact menu labels and download buttons have not yet been verified in your account; use the grouping and column requirements above to identify the exports.

Upload the report pair

  1. Choose a start date on or before the earliest VA purchase you want to assess, and an end date through which you want results.
  2. Export both reports for that same complete range, UK marketplace and GBP. Use the original CSV or tab-separated TXT files.
  3. Select both files below and enter the dates used in SellerToolKit.

Each successful upload replaces the shared sales dataset. Include earlier history again when extending the end date. Uploading only the latest month would remove earlier sales coverage.

VA expectations and purchasing history
