A trusted, auto-priced candidate that also clears 25%+ ROI is added straight to the Review Queue automatically -- no further action needed. Anything found but not auto-trusted, or not profitable enough, stays here for you to review: confirm the price as-is, or replace the retailer entirely with a better one you find yourself (see "other retailers found" on each row) -- either way recalculates profit/ROI and adds it to the Review Queue if it now qualifies. Automatically searches the web for a UK retailer selling products the Sourcing classifier couldn't explain as A2A -- so you don't have to Google each one by hand. Checks up to 3 search queries and 3 candidate retailers per product.

An ASIN already searched in the last 7 days won't be searched again automatically, to avoid wasting search budget. Atlas also skips anything with less than £3 of theoretical margin at the usual 25% ROI bar, even if the math works on paper -- not a realistic purchase once you account for real-world slippage.

Match confidence, strongest to weakest: EAN > Brand+MPN > MPN alone > Brand+Title > Title-only. Title-only is never a confirmed match -- always verify it yourself. See match confidence tiers.

The SerpApi search counter (top right) shows how many Google Shopping searches remain on your plan this month -- a run stops early to protect that quota if it's running low.
