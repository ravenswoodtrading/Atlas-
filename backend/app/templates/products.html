A browsable, historical log of every ASIN Atlas has ever scanned -- from Discovery, the Scan Queue, Watchlist, or Replen -- one row per ASIN, showing its most recent scan only (re-scanning something doesn't add a second row).

Today's Scans vs. Historical Scans: "Today" is everything scanned since UTC midnight; "Historical" is everything before that, including today's -- it's the full log, not "everything except today."

Score (0-100) is how good the opportunity is. Confidence (0-100) is how much to trust that score -- docked for price swings, offer-count spikes, or thin sales evidence. BUY needs score 85+ and confidence 80+; CONSIDER needs 65+/60+. Rows with confirmed sales evidence and over 25% ROI (today or on the 90-day typical price) are highlighted green -- see the star buy definition.

A red GATED badge means a genuinely good opportunity on a brand you're currently blocked from selling -- see Excluded vs. Gated and Gated Opportunities.
