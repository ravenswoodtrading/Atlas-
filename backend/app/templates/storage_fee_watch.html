Pulls Amazon's monthly storage-fee report and long-term/aged-inventory surcharge report for every FBA SKU, joins in current stock and recent units sold, and ranks everything by this month's storage fee -- highest first.

Surcharge means Amazon's long-term storage fee report shows a nonzero charge for this SKU right now -- it's already sat in a fulfilment centre long enough to cost extra, however Amazon currently defines that threshold. Atlas deliberately never hardcodes a day count here, since Amazon's own rules for this have changed more than once -- whichever charge bucket the live report shows is what's shown.

Consider shifting means the storage fee is high relative to how little has actually sold in the recent lookback window -- a candidate for a price drop, a promotion, or removal, rather than paying to keep it sitting in a fulfilment centre. This page never acts on any of this automatically -- it's a ranked view to work from manually in Seller Central.
