This page re-checks live prices every time you load it -- unlike Products (a historical log), Watchlist always shows today's real Keepa data, which is why it spends tokens and shows a "Keepa tokens remaining" count.

By default the table only shows rows Atlas currently scores BUY, CONSIDER, PEAK_WINDOW, or GATED -- anything scoring IGNORE is hidden (use "Show all" top-right to see them anyway).

The amber "Don't rule out" note under a 90-day figure means that row is only profitable at a recent price peak, not today or on average -- but genuinely on enough real days (not just a one-off spike) to be worth watching. See "Today" vs "90d" vs "Peak".

A weekly background job also re-checks anything nobody's manually rescanned in 7 days, and the same job quietly removes an auto-added item once it's had a genuine, repeated chance (14+ days, 2+ real rechecks) to go profitable and never has -- a manual "Watch" or an OOS-restock watch is never removed this way, only the app's own speculative auto-adds.
