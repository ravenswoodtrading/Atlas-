Sold revenue: {{ money(sold_cohort.revenue) }} · Sold cost: {{ money(sold_cohort.cost) }}. Expected profit is the VA's expected profit per unit multiplied by units sold. Profit difference = (actual − expected) ÷ expected × 100; a positive figure means above plan. Percentage difference needs positive expected profit. ROI = actual profit ÷ sold cost × 100. Margin = actual profit ÷ sold revenue × 100. Both use totals, not averages of lead percentages. Partially sold leads contribute only their sold units.
