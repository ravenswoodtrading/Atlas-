Gated Brands ({{ gated_brands|length }})

Brands (or brand+category combos) you can't currently get Amazon approval to sell. Unlike the exclusions above, Atlas doesn't just throw these away: brand-search scanning of a gated brand is skipped entirely to avoid wasting tokens hunting for more of it, but if one turns up anyway -- most commonly via Competitor Watch spotting a rival selling it -- it still gets fully priced and scored, just tagged "Gated" instead of a normal recommendation. See Gated Brand Opportunities to build a case for which brands are worth pursuing ungating on.

Excluded Brands ({{ excluded_brands|length }})

Brands you've decided to stop scanning/tracking altogether, for any reason OTHER than Amazon approval (that's Gated Brands, above). Different from gating: an excluded brand is removed from the Scan Queue immediately, and Scan Intelligence's Attention Engine will never recommend it. A brand-search scan of an excluded brand is skipped before any Keepa token is spent, same as a gated one.
