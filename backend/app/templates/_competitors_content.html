New listings detected on rival sellers you're tracking, auto-tagged with a best-GUESS sourcing method and split into tabs. See Tracked Sellers to add or remove who's being watched.

Every tag here is inferred from price history alone, over the last 10 days -- never a confirmed fact, since Keepa has no visibility into anyone's real purchase history. See sourcing tags for the full caveat.

Rough thresholds behind each tag: UK A2A needs a genuine price dip to 70% or less of the 90-day average that's since recovered to 90%+. Wholesale (likely) needs 3 or fewer current sellers and the same brand listed by this seller before. EU A2A is confirmed the same way Discovery scores it -- a real UK price/EU cost spread that clears the usual ROI bar.

The fire icon means 2 or more tracked competitors listed the same ASIN within 14 days -- stronger corroboration than one seller alone.
