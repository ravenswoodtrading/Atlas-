Takes competitor ASINs Atlas's own Sourcing classifier already tagged "OA / unclear" (no recent EU or UK A2A evidence -- see the Competitors page) and tries to automatically find the cheapest genuine UK retailer price for the same product via Google Shopping, cross-checked against the ASIN's brand/EAN/MPN/title before it's trusted (title similarity alone is never enough -- see the Match column). A trusted, auto-priced candidate that also clears 25%+ ROI is added straight to the Review Queue automatically -- no further action needed. Anything found but not auto-trusted, or not profitable enough, stays here for you to review: confirm the price as-is, or replace the retailer entirely with a better one you find yourself (see "other retailers found" on each row) -- either way recalculates profit/ROI and adds it to the Review Queue if it now qualifies. A handful of harder cases still fall back to the older Brave web-search pipeline, which only finds a retailer page (no price) for you to confirm manually. Batches always work through the "Next up to scan" list below in best-opportunity-first order, and each run automatically stops spending SerpApi searches once your remaining monthly quota gets low (falling back to Brave instead) so one big batch can't quietly burn through the whole month.
Spends ~1 Keepa token per ASIN (a fresh full lookup) plus 1 SerpApi Google Shopping search per ASIN, and only falls back to a few extra Brave searches per ASIN when Shopping doesn't find a trusted match. A 100-ASIN run can take several minutes -- the page will redirect once it's done.
