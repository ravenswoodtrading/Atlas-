The seller IDs Atlas checks for new listings, three times a day, feeding the Competitor Storefront Watch page. Nothing here surfaces NEW sellers to track automatically -- you add each one manually (by Amazon seller ID), usually after spotting them as a competitor on an ASIN you already know.

Seller types -- how a competitor sources, so you can look at EU A2A sellers and OA sellers separately:

  • EU A2A -- mostly buys from Amazon DE/FR/ES/IT and resells in the UK.
  • OA / Wholesale + A2A -- does both: a real share of EU A2A alongside online arbitrage and wholesale.
  • OA / Wholesale and OA -- sources from UK retailers and trade suppliers.

Some products can never be EU A2A: anything with a mains plug (hoovers, kettles, electricals), laptops. A seller whose range is full of them has to be sourced OA whatever the EU price data says, so the suggestion counts how much of a seller's range looks like plug/electrical items. A seller that has lots of those and real EU A2A on the rest is suggested as "does both".

The suggestion is only a suggestion -- it comes from the sourcing tags Atlas has already given each seller's detections, and a seller needs at least 20 classified detections before it makes one. You choose the type; the "Sort ... from the evidence" button fills in only sellers you haven't sorted yet and never overrides one you have. Because some sellers do both, the Does EU A2A and Does OA / Wholesale views overlap: a mixed seller appears in each.
