How these results are calculated

For each ASIN, sales on or after the purchase date go to the oldest eligible VA purchase until its purchased quantity is filled. If you bought 10 units in May and 10 in June, the first 10 qualifying sales from May onward fill the May batch, and the next 10 fill June. Profit follows those same oldest-first allocations. Existing stock may contribute to these matched sales. Remaining units are units not yet matched to sales; they are not a statement of current Amazon inventory. A daily export cannot identify individual transactions or the order of sales within a day. Partial rows share sales and profit proportionally by units.

Purchase dates come from the VA sheet or a single Buy Sheet purchase between this lead and its next submission. Ambiguous dates, incomplete coverage and returns/adjustments are shown for review, not scored. Re-upload a complete date range starting on or before the earliest VA purchase you want to assess; each ad hoc upload replaces the previous exports.

Target judgements use your 30-day, 25% ROI or 14% margin rule. Missing costs may prevent a financial judgement.
